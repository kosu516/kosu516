# Hi, I'm Si-Xian Hsu

I'm an Electrical Engineering undergraduate at National Taiwan University (NTU) interested in robotics, reinforcement learning, and computer vision.

## Selected Projects

### [Turkish Ice Cream Robot](https://github.com/work-tomorrow/turkish-ice-cream)
**Best Maker Award · makeNTU 2026**  
Our team built a 3D-printed, Raspberry Pi–based robotic arm that uses hand tracking to mimic a Turkish ice cream vendor. I primarily contributed to mechanical design, hardware integration, and the hand-tracking algorithm.

### [Pose-Guided Proportion Assessment for Illustrated Characters](https://github.com/ML-1141/pose-guided-proportional-assessment)
Our team combined skeletal alignment and a Siamese CNN to assess proportions in 2D character illustrations, using Grad-CAM to visualize which regions influenced the model’s comparisons.

### [LightDance Firmware](https://github.com/YuHsuan0116/LightDance_Firmware)
I worked on the playback module of ESP-based firmware for synchronized LED lighting in dance performances, focusing on frame rendering, playback timing, and state-machine control.

### [Monopoly Game Website](https://github.com/liftleaf1105/camp_monopoly)
I extended an existing full-stack Monopoly app with new game mechanics, end-of-game asset calculations, and admin tools. I also added automated backups and improved the mobile interface.

### [Interactive Wind-Instrument Practice Device](https://github.com/kosu516/113-2-car-car-final)
Our team built a portable, silent practice device with glove-mounted fingering sensors and a head-mounted airflow sensor. The ESP-based prototype supports three interactive practice modes.
